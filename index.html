01 / About Lamont Roofing Services

Over 65 Years of Roofing Experience. Family Run Since 1975.

Led by Managing Director Paul Lamont and registered as Lamont Roofing Services Pty Ltd since 1 July 2007 (ABN 97 125 608 942), our business carries forward a family roofing tradition established in 1975 and backed by more than 65 years of hands-on industry knowledge.

Based in Menai and Illawong in the Sutherland Shire, we specialise in industrial and commercial roofing — from small-scale remedial repairs to full large-span warehouse new roofs and re-roofs — while continuing to support residential homeowners with dependable roof repairs and restorations across Greater Sydney.

03 / Specialised Commercial Capabilities

Single-Point Capability From Roof Sheet to Stormwater Line

Commercial facilities rarely have simple single-trade issues. Lamont Roofing Services integrates re-roofing, cooling, access safety, concrete overflow core drilling, and stormwater line clearing under one experienced roof.

Re-Roofing & Fibro Conversion
Capability Spotlight 01

Large-Scale Metal & Fibro (Asbestos) Re-Roofing

Ageing fibro (asbestos) or corroded industrial roof sheeting puts stock, tenants, and structural purlins at risk. We plan and execute complete small-to-large scale commercial roof replacements with clear staging and durable steel profiles.

  • New commercial roofs and complete re-roofs for warehouses and factories
  • Specialist metal and fibro (asbestos) roof replacement planning
  • Coordinated roof & rainwater supplies, skylights, and perimeter flashings
Warehouse Cooling & Roof Safety
Capability Spotlight 02

Evaporative Warehouse Cooling, Rotor Vents & Roof Access Safety

Keep high-bay industrial spaces ventilated and compliant for ongoing rooftop servicing. We supply and install heavy-duty wall-mounted evaporative air conditioning units alongside rotary vents and safe rooftop access walkways.

  • Wall-mounted Evaporative Air Conditioning units for large warehouses & indoor stadiums
  • Commercial Rotor Vents and daylighting skylight replacements
  • Roof access & safety systems supplied and installed for future maintenance
CCTV Drainage & Remedial Diagnostics
Capability Spotlight 03

Box Gutters, Stormwater CCTV Inspections, Water Jetting & Core Drilling

Many recurring commercial roof leaks actually start below the roofline — inside undersized box gutters, missing overflows, or blocked stormwater pipes. Our technicians diagnose and resolve the full water path.

  • Stormwater drain CCTV inspections & high-pressure water jetting of blocked lines
  • Concrete core drilling for roof overflows, box gutter & downpipe upgrades
  • Purchase inspections, remedial condition reports, roof cleaning & maintenance schedules
